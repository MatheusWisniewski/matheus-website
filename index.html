About me

I'm a Full Stack Software Engineer in Google DeepMind's GenAI unit, crafting UIs for AI interaction platforms like Google AI Studio — tools that let researchers probe state-of-the-art models and let developers everywhere experiment with Gemini.

Despite working on highly technical problems, I relate very well with people and enjoy presenting to large audiences. An entrepreneurial streak drives me to keep seeking innovative ways to solve problems.

My path spans control & automation engineering, optics, airport systems, fintech dashboards, YouTube partnerships and now generative AI — and next, an MSc in Integrated Machine Learning Systems at UCL.
